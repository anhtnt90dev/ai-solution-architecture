## LinkedIn Post

Most AI systems do not fail because the model cannot answer.

They fail because the architecture boundaries are unclear:

- agent vs workflow ownership
- runtime vs retrieval responsibility
- prompt changes without evaluation gates
- tool calls without governance
- traces without lineage
- RAG without data contracts

I put together a bilingual English/Vietnamese AI Solution Architecture knowledge system covering 17 repositories across agents, serving, RAG, training, LLMOps, MCP, and production governance.

It includes a course path, deep dives, templates, a capstone, and assessment rubrics.
